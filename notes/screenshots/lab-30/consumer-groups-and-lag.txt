Lab 30 consumer groups and lag, captured 2026-08-11
examples\lab30-crm

crm-notifications competing, crm-audit independent, on the same topic.


ASSIGNMENT, TWO MEMBERS IN crm-notifications

  docker exec crm-kafka /opt/kafka/bin/kafka-consumer-groups.sh --bootstrap-server localhost:9092 --describe --group crm-notifications --members

  GROUP              CONSUMER-ID              #PARTITIONS
  crm-notifications  console-consumer-0211db  2
  crm-notifications  console-consumer-111e16  1

Three partitions across two members, 2 and 1, no partition assigned twice.
Adding the second member triggered a rebalance and the first gave one up.


COMPETING VS INDEPENDENT, SIX RECORDS PUBLISHED

  keys CUS-1001 and CUS-1002 to partition 0, CUS-1003 twice to 1,
  CUS-1005 twice to 2

  member holding partition 2         received   2 records
  member holding partitions 0 and 1  received   4 records
  crm-audit                          received  12 records

The six split across the two crm-notifications members with no overlap.
crm-audit is a different group with its own offsets, so it read every record on
the topic including the ones crm-notifications had already consumed.


LAG WITH THE CONSUMER STOPPED

  three more records published while crm-audit had no members

  docker exec crm-kafka /opt/kafka/bin/kafka-consumer-groups.sh --bootstrap-server localhost:9092 --describe --group crm-audit

  Consumer group 'crm-audit' has no active members.
  GROUP      PARTITION  CURRENT-OFFSET  LOG-END-OFFSET  LAG
  crm-audit  1          4               5               1
  crm-audit  2          4               5               1
  crm-audit  0          8               9               1


AFTER RESTARTING THE CONSUMER

  Partition:1  CUS-1003  {"eventId":"d0f4a5b2-...0022",...}
  Partition:2  CUS-1005  {"eventId":"d0f4a5b2-...0023",...}
  Partition:0  CUS-1001  {"eventId":"d0f4a5b2-...0021",...}
  Processed a total of 3 messages

  GROUP      PARTITION  CURRENT-OFFSET  LOG-END-OFFSET  LAG
  crm-audit  1          5               5               0
  crm-audit  2          5               5               0
  crm-audit  0          9               9               0

Lag is log end offset minus committed offset, per partition and per group.
Nothing was lost while the consumer was down, the records sat on the log and
the group resumed from its committed position. Retention is the deadline on
that, not the group.
