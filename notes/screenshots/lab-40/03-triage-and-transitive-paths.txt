WHY TWO ROWS ARE false_positive, WITH EVIDENCE

lab40-007  log4j-api 2.23.1, 4 CVEs at 7.5
  log4j-core is not on the classpath. The tree shows only log4j-to-slf4j >
  log4j-api; Boot routes logging through the slf4j bridge into Logback. The
  matched CPE cpe:2.3:a:apache:log4j:2.23.1 covers the log4j product as a
  whole, and these CVEs need the core implementation that is absent.

lab40-008  angus-activation 2.0.2, CVE-2025-7962 at 7.5
  CPE mismatch. Artifact is org.eclipse.angus:angus-activation; matched CPE is
  cpe:2.3:a:eclipse:angus_mail - a different product. angus-activation is the
  JAF activation implementation, angus-mail is the mail library, and this
  application sends no mail.

Neither rationale is "not exploitable". Each names the classpath fact or the
CPE mismatch a reviewer can check independently.

DOUBLE COUNTING NOTED
  spring-boot and spring-boot-starter-web report the same 5 CVEs under the same
  CPE, as do spring-core/spring-web and spring-security-core/spring-security-web.
