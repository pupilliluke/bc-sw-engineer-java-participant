Lab 32 actuator observation, captured 2026-08-13
examples\lab32-crm

The starter has no controller, so a property-guarded runner drives the calls.
AccountDemoRunner runs only with --lab.demo=true. It makes 5 calls to find()
against the default base-url http://localhost:8089 with nothing listening
there, waits 2.5s, then makes 2 more.

  mvn -B spring-boot:run "-Dspring-boot.run.arguments=--server.port=8092 --lab.demo=true"


  curl -s http://localhost:8092/actuator/health

  {"status":"UP","components":{"circuitBreakers":{"status":"UNKNOWN","details":{
    "accountProfile":{"status":"CIRCUIT_OPEN","details":{
      "failureRate":"100.0%","failureRateThreshold":"50.0%",
      "bufferedCalls":2,"failedCalls":2,"state":"OPEN"}}}},
    "diskSpace":{"status":"UP"},"ping":{"status":"UP"}}}

The breaker state is in health via registerHealthIndicator: true plus
management.health.circuitbreakers.enabled: true. The overall status stays UP,
CIRCUIT_OPEN is reported on the component rather than failing the whole app.


  curl -s http://localhost:8092/actuator/circuitbreakers

  {"circuitBreakers":{"accountProfile":{
    "failureRate":"100.0%","slowCallRate":"0.0%",
    "failureRateThreshold":"50.0%","slowCallRateThreshold":"100.0%",
    "bufferedCalls":2,"failedCalls":2,"slowCalls":0,"slowFailedCalls":0,
    "notPermittedCalls":0,"state":"OPEN"}}}

failureRateThreshold is 50.0%, the value from application.yml rather than a
default, which is the check for an instance name in the annotation that
matches nothing in the config. bufferedCalls is 2 rather than 6 because the
window resets on the transition to HALF_OPEN, the 2 calls are the failed
probes.


  curl -s http://localhost:8092/actuator/circuitbreakerevents

  10:30:53.096  ERROR             TemporaryAccountException, 176ms
  10:30:53.114  ERROR             6ms
  10:30:53.122  ERROR             6ms
  10:30:53.130  ERROR             6ms
  10:30:53.131  FAILURE_RATE_EXCEEDED
  10:30:53.138  STATE_TRANSITION  CLOSED_TO_OPEN
  10:30:53.141  NOT_PERMITTED
  10:30:55.657  STATE_TRANSITION  OPEN_TO_HALF_OPEN
  10:30:55.662  ERROR             4ms
  10:30:55.669  ERROR             5ms
  10:30:55.669  STATE_TRANSITION  HALF_OPEN_TO_OPEN

Four errors open the breaker, not five. minimumNumberOfCalls is 4, so the rate
is computed at the fourth call, 100% crosses the 50% threshold, and the fifth
call is NOT_PERMITTED. After waitDurationInOpenState 2s the next call moves
the breaker to HALF_OPEN, both probes fail against the dead port and it goes
back to OPEN.

The successful recovery, HALF_OPEN to CLOSED, is asserted in
halfOpen_successfulProbesCloseTheBreaker, see 01-mvn-test.txt.
