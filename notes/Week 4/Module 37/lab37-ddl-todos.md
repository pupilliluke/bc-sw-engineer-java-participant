## Step 2 — Fill

`VARCHAR(200)` over TEXT for full_name, both are the same storage in
PostgreSQL and the length states an intent the next reader can see.
`now()` as the created_at default so the timestamp comes from the database
clock and not from whichever machine ran the insert. the status list is
the four from the constraints note. the FK references
`customer(customer_id)`, the column named there is the parent's key, not
the child's. Amina Khan and Ravi Singh as the two seed names.

the seed above sets both to ACTIVE because that is what the starter line
holds. Ravi is PROSPECT in the fixtures, so the lab's own seed uses
PROSPECT for CUS-1002 and gives Amina the account row.

## Step 4 — Dialect

PostgreSQL only. no `NUMBER`, no `VARCHAR2`, no `CASCADE CONSTRAINTS
PURGE`, no `SEQUENCE` plus trigger for the id. `GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS
IDENTITY` is the standard-SQL identity column PostgreSQL supports, which
is also what Oracle 12c and later use, so the id line is the one piece
here that ports as written.
