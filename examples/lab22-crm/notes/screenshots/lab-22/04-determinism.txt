The two new tests write into the same in-memory store the guide's
troubleshooting table warns about, so both use ids that are not seeded and not
used by any other class. CustomerServiceTest builds its own
InMemoryCustomerRepository with new and gets a fresh map per run.
CustomerServiceSpringTest shares the singleton, so CUS-2202 stays in the map
for the rest of that context; it is created once and read once in the same
method and nothing else looks for it.

CustomerServiceSpringTest is @SpringBootTest with no webEnvironment, so its
context is cached separately from the four RANDOM_PORT IT classes. That is why
CustomerService ready appears twice across a full verify and once per context.
