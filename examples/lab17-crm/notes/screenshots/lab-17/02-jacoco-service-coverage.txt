THE BRANCH THAT WAS RED LAST

customer.getFullName().isBlank()). The first full run reported "1 of 4 branches
missed" on that line: blank was covered by blankFullNameIsA400NamingTheField and
null was not. aMissingFullNameIsA400LikeABlankOne closed it and took the service
package from 19 of 20 branches to 20 of 20. Lines were already 46 of 46 at that
point, so the gate was green before the branch was covered; the HTML report is
what showed it.
