Windows, Temurin JDK 21.0.4, Maven 3.9.9, examples\lab21-crm

App on port 8080. A CrmApplication left running from the lab 20 session held

1, PROBES BEFORE ANY TRAFFIC

  curl -s http://localhost:8080/actuator/health

  {"status":"UP","components":{"crmReadinessIndicator":{"status":"UP","details":{"crm":"ready"}},"diskSpace":{"status":"UP","details":{"total":999343255552,"free":349536215040,"threshold":10485760,"path":"C:\Users\lukel\java-bootcamp\examples\lab21-crm\.","exists":true}},"livenessState":{"status":"UP"},"ping":{"status":"UP"},"readinessState":{"status":"UP"}},"groups":["liveness","readiness"]}

  curl -s http://localhost:8080/actuator/health/liveness

  {"status":"UP"}

  curl -s http://localhost:8080/actuator/health/readiness

  {"status":"UP","components":{"crmReadinessIndicator":{"status":"UP","details":{"crm":"ready"}},"readinessState":{"status":"UP"}}}

crmReadinessIndicator appears in the overall blob and in the readiness group,
not in liveness. groups lists both, which is what application.yml asks for. The
diskSpace details are the reason show-details: always is marked lab-only: the
absolute project path and the free bytes go to any caller.


2, COUNTERS BEFORE ANY TRAFFIC

  curl -s http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/crm.customer.create

  HTTP 404

  curl -s http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/crm.customer.get

  HTTP 404

Micrometer registers a counter when Counter.builder(...).register(registry)
first runs, which is inside recordCreate and recordGet. Before any traffic
neither has been called, so the meter does not exist and the endpoint 404s
rather than reporting zero. 404 here is the correct baseline, not a fault.

  curl -s http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/crm.customer.get

  curl -s http://localhost:8080/actuator/metrics/crm.customer.get.latency

  {"name":"crm.customer.get.latency","baseUnit":"seconds","measurements":[{"statistic":"COUNT","value":1.0},{"statistic":"TOTAL_TIME","value":5.633E-4},{"statistic":"MAX","value":5.633E-4}],"availableTags":[{"tag":"application","values":["northstar-crm"]}]}

One POST and one GET, one increment each. availableTags carries result and
application and nothing else; application comes from management.metrics.tags
and is one fixed value. 0.56 milliseconds against an in-memory map, which is
the honest number here, and the figure experiment 4 moves.
