THE TWO

forceFailRollsBack reads the MAIN balance and logs.count(), calls transfer to
ACC-FORCE-FAIL, asserts the throw, then asserts the balance compares equal to
before and the log count is unchanged.

happyPathMovesFunds reads both balances and the log count, transfers 5.00
MAIN to LOYALTY, then asserts MAIN down 5.00, LOYALTY up 5.00 and one more row.

Both read their before values instead of hard-coding 1000.00 and 50.00, so
neither depends on running first. BigDecimal is compared with compareTo rather
than equals, since 950.00 and 950.0 are equal in value and not in scale.

The whole suite shares one H2 database and AccountSeed runs once, so absolute
assertions would have coupled the two tests to their order.
