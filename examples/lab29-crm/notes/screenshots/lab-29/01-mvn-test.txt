THE FOUR

validationReturns400Envelope posts an empty id, an empty name and not-an-email
with X-Correlation-Id: lab-request-001, and asserts 400, error Bad Request,
message Validation failed, correlationId lab-request-001, violations is an array
of at least 3 and contains the field email.

missingCustomerReturns404Envelope gets CUS-9999 and asserts 404, error Not
Found, and that the message contains CUS-9999.

duplicateReturns409Envelope posts CUS-1001 again with a valid body and asserts
409, error Conflict, and that the message contains CUS-1001.

securityStillRequiresToken gets CUS-1001 with no Authorization header and
asserts 401.

The first three call POST /api/auth/login as agent1 and send the token. Security
stays on for all four.

VIOLATIONS ARE ASSERTED WITHOUT AN INDEX

The assert is violations[*].field contains email and length at least 3, not
violations[0].field equals email. Bean Validation does not promise an order for
field errors. The handler sorts them by field name, and the test does not depend
on that sort holding.
